---
title: UNC-CH AAUP Statement on UNC’s Agreement with the Department of Justice
---

In mid-October 2026 UNC announced a deal with the Department of
Justice (DoJ) via [a press
release](https://www.unc.edu/posts/2026/10/19/carolina-reaches-agreement-with-federal-government-to-resolve-civil-rights-investigations/).
In the press release Chancellor Lee Roberts claimed, “We’re proud to
resolve [a range of] issues at Carolina in a way that protects our
mission and affirms the civil rights of everyone in the community.”

Regardless of UNC’s motives or the nature of these Title VI
complaints, the Chapel Hill chapter of the AAUP does not see this
agreement as a proud moment for our University, nor do we think the
agreement affirms the civil rights of all of our students.

We stand with student groups and alumni who have also expressed grave
concerns over how this agreement will be implemented over the next two
years. We aim to be vigilant in resisting any attempt at enforcement
by UNC and the DoJ that violates the 1964 Civil Rights Act, creates an
unsafe environment, or restricts academic freedom.
